About me

Hi, I'm Elena! I'm a Creative Engineer currently pursuing my Master's degree in Human Factors Engineering at TUM.

I developed proBricks as my bachelor's thesis, and one of the things I enjoyed most was how many different areas it brought together. I designed the bricks and their custom PCBs, programmed the firmware and communication system, and developed the frontend with its interactive 3D visualization.

What made the project even more special to me was that I was developing it for children with special educational needs. I had the chance to visit their school and let them try the first prototype. Seeing how much fun they had building and experimenting with something I had created was probably my favorite moment of the whole project.

That's exactly the kind of work I enjoy most: combining creativity and technology to make something that people actually enjoy using.
